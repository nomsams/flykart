    Route provenance · loading

    The population button runs all configured brains simultaneously, including 10+ cars. The first generation explores many variants; after evaluation, the protected incumbent mates with the best-progress candidate for the configured share (80% by default) and the runner-up for the remainder. Choose one interaction mode: shared physical evolution uses rigid collisions; Independent evolution gives every brain its own world; Ghost contact mode keeps one shared visual race so brains sense and penalize overlap, while contact remains non-blocking.
