SONAR · HC-SR04What the ping hears
—

Blue = scanned approach, amber = uncertain echo band, dark = unobserved. Repeated returns strengthen memory. No echo is unknown. Marker height is illustrative. HC-SR04 sonar, not a rotating lidar; this diagnostic uses simulator pose and is excluded from neural inputs.

One ping about every 66 ms, a beam roughly 15° wide that returns the nearest echo. It sees cones, barriers, other karts, trees and rocks, but not paint, kerbs, oil or ponds (flat things the sound skims over), and a thin or slanted target can fade away. The two numbers the controller gets are how close the echo is and how strong it was.
