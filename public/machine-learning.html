FROM TRACK SENSORS TO SPIKES TO STEERING

How FlyKart learns

FlyKart is a compact evolutionary machine-learning experiment. A recurrent spiking network reads nine numerical sensors, chooses four driving controls, and improves by keeping the brains that master more of the route.

01 · THE LOOP

One decision, every simulation tick

The same deterministic pipeline runs in visual training, headless training, and races. Rendering can speed up or slow down without changing the physics timestep.

  1. 1Sense

    Measure track geometry, car motion, road position, and nearby traffic.

  2. 2Spike

    Integrate the inputs and recurrent activity across 48 leaky neurons.

  3. 3Act

    Decode activity into steering, throttle, brake, and reverse.

  4. 4Score

    Reward route mastery and safe forward motion; penalize failure modes.

  5. 5Evolve

    Retain the incumbent, recombine strong parents, mutate, and evaluate again.

02 · THE BRAIN

A tiny recurrent spiking controller

Each cell remembers a decaying voltage. Current sensor input and last tick’s spikes can push it past threshold. A spike resets its voltage and contributes to the four motor outputs.

03 · TRAINING

Evolution, not backpropagation

FlyKart does not differentiate through the physics engine. It evaluates complete networks, selects by route mastery, and searches the parameter space with protected elites, crossover, and mutation.

01

Protect the incumbent

The strongest known brain is cloned exactly into slot one. A weaker generation cannot erase it.

02

Recombine and mutate

Short blocks of weights come from selected parents. Gaussian noise changes a sampled fraction of parameters.

03

Escape plateaus

When improvement stalls, mutation pressure rises and deterministic random immigrants add diversity.

SELECTION ORDERCompleted lapthenRoute coveragethenReward

Raw reward is only the tie-breaker. This prevents a brain from collecting local shaping rewards forever while failing to solve the route.

05 · SIMULATION VS REALITY

The reality gap matters

A controller that performs in FlyKart has learned this simulator’s numerical world. A physical kart would introduce perception, contact dynamics, sensor error, actuator delays, compute limits, and safety constraints that are absent here.

SIMULATOR

Clean and controlled

  • Exact compact sensor vector
  • Deterministic fixed-step physics
  • Repeatable obstacle seeds
  • Fast parallel evaluation
reality gap→
PHYSICAL WORLD

Noisy and delayed

  • Cameras, calibration, and occlusion
  • Friction and contact uncertainty
  • Actuator lag and hardware jitter
  • Safety-critical failures
Practical transfer pattern

Randomize physics and observations in simulation, train across many conditions, validate on held-out scenarios, collect real logs, fine-tune with real data, and deploy gradually behind monitoring and safe fallbacks.
